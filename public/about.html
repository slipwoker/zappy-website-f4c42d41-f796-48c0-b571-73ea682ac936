About the Studio

Creative clarity,
crafted with care.

Lior Design is a boutique graphic design studio built on the belief that great design begins with deep listening. Every project is shaped by a blend of creative intuition and structured thinking — translating your vision into visual work that feels both intentional and alive. Whether it's a brand identity, editorial layout, or digital experience, the process is always personal, always considered, and grounded in genuine care for the outcome.

Over ten years working across brand identity, print, and digital design — building a vocabulary that is refined, flexible, and always human.

Relationships Over Transactions

The most rewarding work comes from clients who value collaboration. Long-term partnerships, honest conversations, and shared creative ownership are what make the work meaningful.

Our Journey

How a Graphic Design Studio Takes Shape Over Time

Lior Design didn't emerge from a business plan — it grew from a genuine love of craft. Every project, every client conversation, and every late-night sketch session has shaped the studio into what it is today: a thoughtful, personal design practice built on curiosity and care.

2014

Where It All Began

What started as freelance identity work out of a small apartment studio quickly revealed a deeper calling. Early clients brought briefs for logos, print collateral, and brand systems — and each project became an opportunity to listen carefully, ask better questions, and deliver work that felt genuinely considered rather than merely competent.

2018

Finding a Specialisation

As the portfolio deepened, a clear focus emerged: brand identity and editorial design for independent businesses, cultural organisations, and boutique agencies. This wasn't a strategic pivot — it was a natural alignment between the kind of work that felt most alive and the clients who valued a slower, more intentional creative process.

Today

A Studio with Direction

Lior Design now operates as a focused graphic design studio serving clients who believe that good design is a long-term investment. The practice remains deliberately boutique — small enough to care deeply about every brief, experienced enough to bring clarity and confidence to even the most complex creative challenges.

Our Beliefs

Design shaped by intention,
guided by principle

At Lior Design, every creative decision is rooted in a set of core beliefs. These aren't rules — they're the quiet convictions that shape how we approach every brief, every collaboration, and every detail that earns its place on the page.

01

Clarity Over Clutter

Good design communicates without effort. We strip away anything that competes for attention, leaving only what genuinely serves the message. Restraint isn't a limitation — it's one of the most powerful tools in a graphic design studio's vocabulary.

02

Emotion With Intention

Every colour, curve, and composition carries feeling. We make those feelings deliberate — choosing visual language that resonates with your audience on a human level while staying true to your brand's unique voice and purpose.

03

Details That Earn Attention

The finest work reveals itself slowly. We invest in the micro-decisions — the spacing between letters, the weight of a line, the texture of a surface — because thoughtful details are what separate memorable design from the merely competent.

04

Collaboration Built on Trust

The best creative work emerges from genuine partnership. We listen deeply before we sketch, ask the questions others skip, and keep you close throughout the process — so the final result feels authentically yours, not just ours.

Where Every Project
Finds Its Form

The studio is a quiet, focused place — a single desk surrounded by printed references, dog-eared books, and material swatches that inform every decision. Work here moves slowly by design: ideas are sketched by hand before they reach a screen, and each concept is tested against real paper and ink before anything is considered resolved. It's a graphic design studio built around deliberate attention rather than rapid output.

01

Process Materials

Sketches, printed proofs, and reference swatches fill the early stages — tangible anchors that keep digital work grounded in real-world feeling.

02

Refinement Sessions

Details are worked through slowly: kerning, colour temperature, paper weight. Each round of review sharpens the work until nothing feels accidental.

03

Final Presentation

Deliverables arrive carefully packaged — whether digital or printed — with context notes that explain every choice so clients feel confident using the work.

Collaborating with Lior Design means being kept genuinely informed at every stage. Expect clear communication, honest timelines, and work that reflects your vision as much as the studio's craft.
