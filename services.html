Questions & Answers

Everything you might want to know

A few things clients often ask before we begin working together — answered honestly and without the jargon.

How long does a typical project take?

Most projects run between two and six weeks depending on scope and how quickly feedback rounds move. Brand identity work tends to take longer than a single deliverable — I'll always share a realistic timeline during our first conversation so there are no surprises.

How does the revision process work?

Every project includes structured revision rounds built into the schedule — usually two to three, depending on the package. Rather than open-ended back-and-forth, we work in focused cycles so feedback stays clear and the design moves forward with intention rather than drifting.

What kinds of businesses do you work with?

Lior Design works best with small to mid-sized businesses, independent professionals, and creative founders who care deeply about how their brand looks and feels. Whether you're launching something new or refreshing an existing identity, the work is most rewarding when the client values craft as much as I do.

Can the scope change once we've started?

Yes — creative work rarely stays perfectly static. If a project grows in a meaningful direction, we'll discuss what that means for timeline and budget before anything changes. Small pivots are part of the process; larger additions are handled transparently through a simple scope update.

How do we get started?

The simplest step is reaching out through the contact page. Share a little about your project — what you're working on, what you're hoping to achieve, and any timing that matters. I'll get back to you within one to two business days and we'll take it from there, no pressure and no lengthy intake forms.
