How We Work Together

Collaboration, Clarity,
and Calm Guidance

Every project at Lior Design begins with listening. Whether you're a small business finding your visual voice or a brand ready for a thoughtful refresh, the process is designed to feel clear, personal, and never overwhelming — from the very first conversation to the final handoff.

01

Discovery

We start with a focused conversation — about your brand, your audience, and what success looks like for you. This isn't a questionnaire; it's a genuine dialogue that shapes everything that follows. Good design begins with deep understanding.

02

Concept Direction

From our discovery, I develop initial creative directions — visual frameworks that explore tone, palette, and form. You'll see clear options, not endless variations. Each concept is intentional and explained, so choosing feels easy rather than arbitrary.

03

Refinement

Once a direction is chosen, we refine together. Feedback is welcomed in focused rounds rather than scattered revisions, keeping the momentum steady and the design sharp. This phase is where the work truly becomes yours.

04

Final Rollout

Delivery is organised and complete — files structured, formats prepared, and guidance included so you can use your new design with confidence. The handoff marks the beginning of your brand's next chapter, not just the end of a project.

Every project is personal.
